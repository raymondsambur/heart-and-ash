# M4: orders, rules and reports

Milestone M4 of `docs/ROADMAP.md`, built on the branch `m4-orders-rules-reports` (from `main` at the merge of M3), written in the owner's main
session. Design inputs: `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` ("You can", "Time and being away") and the server section of `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

Done when: you can set rules, close the game for an evening, come back to a report that explains what happened, and the village did something
sensible in between. All of it works end to end: in the sim tests, through the host with a fake clock, in the in-page mock and in the soak.

## What you get

### Priorities (`shared/data/policy.ts`, `sim/policy.ts`)

Seven kinds of work (food, wood and planks, stone, building, defence, care and burials, tools and cloth) each have a level from Off to Urgent.
Every job on the board belongs to one kind (`groupOfJob`), and the level scales how attractive the job is to the people who choose among
jobs: off 0.15, low 0.6, normal 1, high 1.5, urgent 2.3. People still choose for themselves and eat, sleep and tend the sick first. A test shows
that urgent wood stores more wood than off wood over three days.

### Standing rules

A rule is one condition and one action (`RuleDef`). Up to 12 rules; they are looked at once a game hour, in order, so a later rule wins over an
earlier one, and they keep working when you are away.

- **Conditions** look at a number: days of food, days of winter fuel, people, houses, fields, shrines, watch posts, wall segments, wood, stone,
  planks, average mood, people sick, the season, raiders expected; compared with `<`, `>` or `=`.
- **Actions:** set the rations (normal, tight, strict); set the priority of a kind of work; or order a building. A building rule places the
  order on the nearest good spot to the green (`autoSpot`), one of a kind at a time and not more than once every 3 days.
- A rule speaks in the feed once, when it first changes something ("Rule: if days of food < 7, set the rations tight."), with "(while you were
  away)" when you were.
- **Suggested rules** (`CARETAKER_RULES`, the "Add suggested rules" button): rations follow the food (full above 14 days, tight under 7, strict
  under 3.5), the watch is urgent while raiders are expected, wood is pressed when winter fuel is under 12 days and relaxed over 24, and houses
  (up to 7), watch posts (2), walls (3), shrines (2) and fields (3) are raised as needed.

### Decisions (`sim/offers.ts`)

Refugees now ask: a card in the middle of the screen with the choices ("Take them in" and "Send them on", each with what it costs), and what
the village will do if nobody answers (it takes them in with 12 or more days of food, and sends them on with fewer). Unanswered after 3 days, or
at once while you are away, the village decides, and the report says so. The mechanism (`Offer`, `answer`) is general: the Outside (M5) uses it.

### Reports (`sim/reports.ts`)

- **Daily** (the last 14 are kept), **seasonal** (the last 6) and **While you were away** (the last 3), in the Reports tab of the Manage panel.
- Each says in plain words what changed and why: people (born, arrived, left, came back, the dead with their causes); food from the stock flows
  (`+25 brought in, -34 eaten, -1 spoiled`) and how many days it lasts at the present rations; wood (`+17 cut, -10 burned in the hearths, -30 used
  for planks and building`) and, from autumn, the days of fuel; hearths gone cold; what raiders carried off; jobs done and buildings finished;
  what your rules did; the decisions the village made for you; the events of the period (raids, plague, bad harvests, the Blight, refugees, a
  master); the mood.
- New reports are marked until read. The away report opens by itself as a dialog when you come back.

### Being away (`server/host.ts`, `sim/sim.ts`)

- When the last client of a village disconnects the village is **kept running** (at most 1x, whatever speed you left it at; a paused village
  stays paused) for `AWAY_CAP_HOURS` real hours (default 8, 0 turns it off). It runs on your rules and priorities.
- **The director is mild while you are away:** no raid arrives (a warned raid is pushed back a day at a time, so it waits for you), no plague
  starts, refugees are decided by the village's fallback.
- **Crisis state:** a village that would be lost parks itself (paused) when it is down to 3 people or fewer, or the food has run out, and says so in the feed and the
  report instead of dying unseen.
- At the cap the village waits (paused), is saved and let go from memory. When you come back the village is picked up where it is (or loaded from its
  save), the away report is written and shown.
- **A server restart does not stop it:** villages that were away when the server stopped are picked up again (for another full cap) at start-up,
  unless they were paused, in a crisis or past the cap.
- The village card shows "The village is running on its own" while it is away (seen when you return mid-away from another tab), or the crisis text.

### Client

The **Manage** panel (bottom right, collapsed by default, a badge counts unread reports and open decisions) has three tabs: **Work** (a row of
five buttons per kind of work), **Rules** (the list with an on/off switch, the sentence and a remove button; an editor to add one; the suggested
rules) and **Reports**. Decisions are a card in the middle of the screen; the away report is a dialog.

### Save and protocol

Snapshot **version 5** adds the priorities, rules, decisions, reports, the bases they compare against and the away state; a version 4 save loads
with default priorities, no rules and no reports (the first daily report comes after the first day). Protocol **version 4** adds `priority`, `rule`,
`rule_del`, `answer` and `read`, and `policy` and `reports` in the view and updates. Every message is validated with zod on the server.

## What differs from the spec, and what is not done

- **Rules have one condition each.** "If A and B" is two rules, and since later rules win, a pair can be arranged to act as one. A rule editor with
  several conditions and an "else" is a later refinement.
- **No rule for a decision.** Decisions are answered by hand or by the village's fallback; a rule such as "always take refugees when food is over
  20 days" would need a rule action `answer`. The fallback is that rule for refugees at 12 days.
- **Rules do not touch individual people** (the design says you cannot give a villager a direct command), only the labour, the rations and orders.
- **Priorities do not stop a starving person eating**, by design.
- **The scripted manager of the soak** still places orders by itself (as in M2 and M3); `--rules` runs the village on the suggested rules alone.
- **Away time is real time at 1x.** There is no catch-up simulation: closing the game for an evening is 8 real hours of 1x (80 game days at most),
  which is the cap the design proposed. It is one number to change.
- **Reports are text.** No charts, no per-person history; the causes are in the sentences.
- **Nightly backups and the restart policy** were done with the first deploy (M0); the away villages being resumed after a restart is new.

## Tests

- `m4.test.ts` (19): every job belongs to a priority group and the levels scale the choice; urgent wood moves more wood than off wood; the rule
  metrics; adding, changing and removing rules with their rejections and the limit; a rule that sets rations once and says so; priority rules in
  order; a build rule that orders one building, waits for the cooldown and for the order to finish; a tight-rations rule saves food; the suggested rules
  are all valid and a village left to them for two months (60 game days away) does not fall apart, nothing hostile comes and the away report mentions the rules; the daily report's numbers and
  wording, its events and tones, the season report, reading a report; being away (mild director, the report on return, the crisis state and its
  restart); saves (a version 4 save loads, everything survives a round trip, and a run with rules and an away stretch continues exactly after save
  and load).
- `host.test.ts` (7, with a fake clock and fake connections): the village keeps living at 1x after the manager leaves (not 4x); the rules act while
  away; the cap pauses it and lets it go, and the report is there on return from the save; a cap of zero keeps the old behaviour; a second login
  does not keep the village; a restart resumes an away village and leaves a paused one; the new messages answer with errors for things that are gone.
- `session.test.ts`: the new messages and the policy and reports in the store. `m3.test.ts` was updated for decisions (refugees ask) and
  snapshot version 5. The golden run's digest changed (`e7c7e166:35873` to `3c25244e:38511`) and its facts did not.

How to read them:

- **A village that runs only on the suggested rules does as well as the one with the scripted manager** (100%, 100% and 88% survival at years 1, 3
  and 5, against 100% in all three; an average of 26 people at the end against 22). The rules build the watch and the walls, set the rations and press the
  woodcutters without anybody giving an order.
- **The same seeds are hard both ways** (seeds 1 and 3 on rules, 2 and 8 with the manager): in those the raiders, who grow by one a year, outrun the
  defence the village builds and overrun it 14 to 18 times; they are the villages a watching player would save by building more. One of them falls
  below 6 people in year 5 on rules.
- The rules acted 49 to 54 times in five years in every seed (rations, wood and the occasional building).
- Decisions (refugees) settled by the village without the manager: 0 to 4 a run, always by the fallback.
- Measured on this computer; the bench is as in M3 (a few hundredths of a millisecond per tick for the rules and reports).


## How to try it

- In the page mock (`npm run dev:client`, `http://localhost:5173/?mock=1&seed=3`): open **Manage** in the bottom right. On Work press Urgent for
  Wood; on Rules press "Add suggested rules"; press `3` for 4x and read the Reports tab after a game day (90 real seconds at 4x).
- On the server: close the tab, come back later: the "While you were away" report opens.
- `npm run soak -- --years 5 --seeds 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8 --rules` runs the village on the suggested rules alone.
